Who we are

About HARP

A North Canterbury business based in Amberley. Registered electrical work and Mitsubishi Electric approved heat pumps, from one local team.

HARP Electrical & Heat Pumps is a North Canterbury business, run by Harpal and based in Amberley. We do two things: registered electrical work, and Mitsubishi Electric approved heat pump installation. Doing both is deliberate, because out here most heating jobs need a sparky anyway, and most people would rather deal with one person than two.

Based in the Hurunui, not visiting it

Plenty of firms will tell you they serve North Canterbury. In practice that usually means they will drive up from Christchurch if the job is big enough. That is why small jobs in Cheviot or Waikari can be so hard to get done, and why the travel line on the invoice sometimes rivals the labour.

We are based in Amberley. The Hurunui is not an outlying area we service, it is where we live and work. Waipara is ten minutes away, Leithfield five, Rangiora twenty. That changes what is worth calling us out for.

Mitsubishi Electric approved

We are an approved Mitsubishi Electric installer. Manufacturers only list installers who work to their standard, and it matters for a practical reason: it keeps your warranty intact. An install that does not meet the standard can leave you arguing about cover three winters later, when the unit is out of the box and well out of mind.

It also means we can size and specify properly. A Hyper Core unit for a place that gets real frosts, a ducted system where a wall unit will not reach, ventilation where the actual problem is damp rather than cold.

How we work

  • A price before we start. You get the number up front. No call-out surprises and no padded invoice at the end.
  • We turn up when we said. A booked time, not a vague window. If we are running behind you get a call.
  • Registered and certified. Electrical work tested, signed off and the paperwork handed over.
  • Tidy. We clean up. The place should look better when we leave than when we arrived.
  • Switched on 7 days. Faults do not wait for Monday, so neither do we.
