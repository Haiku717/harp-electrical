Electrician in Waikari & Hawarden

Half an hour from our Amberley base. Switchboard upgrades, heat pumps and farm electrical work for inland Hurunui homes and properties.

Waikari and Hawarden sit inland off the Inland Scenic Route, and like a lot of small Hurunui settlements they are well served for community and badly served for tradesmen. We cover both from Amberley, about half an hour away.

  • Repairs, fault finding and safety checks
  • Farm building, shed and yard lighting
  • New builds, renovations and extensions
  • Security cameras for rural properties

Older homes, older wiring

There are some lovely old houses around Waikari and Hawarden, and quite a few of them are still running switchboards that belong in a museum. Ceramic fuses, no RCD protection, and circuits that were laid out when a house had a fraction of the appliances it has now. Add a heat pump and a dishwasher to that and it starts complaining.

A board upgrade is usually the first thing worth doing. It is a one day job, it makes the house genuinely safer, and it clears the way for anything else you want to add later.

Warmth without a second fire

Most homes out this way run a woodburner, which does a good job on the room it is in and nothing at all for the far end of the house. A heat transfer system is the cheap fix: it takes the heat pooling at the ceiling and pushes it into the cold bedrooms. Where a heat pump makes more sense, we fit Hyper Core units that hold up to inland frosts.

One trip, several jobs

Since we are coming out from Amberley, it pays to get everything looked at in one go. Most people out here line up the board, a few faults and some shed lighting into a single visit rather than booking us three times.
