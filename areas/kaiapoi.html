Rebuilt and original housing side by side

Kaiapoi has an unusual housing mix after the rebuild. Whole streets are near new, built to current standards with modern boards and wiring, and right alongside them are original houses that came through it and are still running everything they always did.

That means very different jobs street to street. On the newer places it is usually heating: a second heat pump, a ducted system, or ventilation. On the older ones it is more often a switchboard upgrade, rewiring or chasing down a fault that has been intermittent for years.

Damp and the river

Being low and close to the water, plenty of Kaiapoi homes deal with more moisture than they should. If your windows run with condensation through winter, that is worth fixing at the source. Ventilation clears it far more effectively than turning the heating up, and it costs less to run.
