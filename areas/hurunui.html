Hurunui district

Based in Amberley, inside the district, covering the Hurunui from the coast to the inland valleys. Registered electrical work and Mitsubishi approved heat pumps.

The Hurunui is a big district with not many electricians in it. Most of the trade serving the area drives up from Christchurch, which means travel charges, long waits and a reluctance to come out for anything small. We are based in Amberley, inside the district, and that is the point.

Towns we cover

Amberley Waipara Leithfield Cheviot Culverden Waikari & Hawarden Hanmer Springs

We also get to the smaller settlements in between: Amberley Beach, Leithfield Beach, Motunau, Greta Valley, Scargill, Rotherham and Waiau. If you are not sure whether we come out your way, just ask.

What we do

  • Heat pump installation and servicing, Mitsubishi Electric approved
  • Ducted heat pumps for whole-home heating and cooling
  • Lossnay ventilation and heat transfer systems

Built for how the district actually works

Rural Hurunui jobs are not the same as town jobs. Long cable runs to outbuildings, three-phase gear in pump and dairy sheds, properties where the shed is a paddock from the house, and older homes carrying wiring from three different decades. That is normal work for us rather than something we have to think about.

Heating that stands up to a Hurunui winter

Inland Hurunui gets genuine frosts, and a lot of people have been put off heat pumps by one that gave up on the coldest morning of the year. That is a unit selection problem, not a heat pump problem. Hyper Core models keep working through it. Where a woodburner is doing the heavy lifting, a heat transfer system is often the cheapest way to get the bedrooms warm.

Electrical and heating from one team

Out here, getting two different trades to turn up on the same project is genuinely hard. We do both, so the wiring, the switchboard capacity and the heating all get sorted together in one visit rather than across three.
