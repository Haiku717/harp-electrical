A district that has grown fast

Waimakariri has taken on a lot of new housing over the last decade, and it shows in the work. New subdivisions in Pegasus, Woodend and around Rangiora are mostly heating jobs: adding a second heat pump, putting in a ducted system, or sorting ventilation. Meanwhile the older housing in Rangiora and Kaiapoi throws up the switchboard upgrades, rewires and fault finding.

Electrical and heating from one team

Most jobs that involve a heat pump also involve electrical work, and most people end up booking two contractors and hoping they turn up in the right order. We are registered for the electrical work and Mitsubishi Electric approved for the heating, so it is one team, one quote and one visit.

Coming from the north

Most of the trade serving Waimakariri comes up from Christchurch. We come down from Amberley, which for the northern half of the district is a good deal closer.
