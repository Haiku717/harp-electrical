A town of two halves

Rangiora has grown fast, and the housing reflects it. There are new subdivisions on the edges where the wiring is modern and the question is mostly which heat pump suits the layout, and there is a good deal of older housing closer in where the switchboard is the first thing worth looking at. We do plenty of both.

On the newer builds, the common job is adding a second heat pump or a ducted system once people realise one unit in the lounge is not reaching the bedrooms. On the older places it is more often a board upgrade, some rewiring and getting the lighting sorted.
