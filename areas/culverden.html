Dairy and irrigation country

The Amuri basin runs on irrigation and dairy, and both come with serious electrical loads. Pump sheds, three-phase supply, motor control and long runs across the property are all part of it. When something goes down mid-season it is not an inconvenience, it costs real money, so getting it diagnosed properly the first time matters more than getting it done cheaply.

Real frosts, real heat

Inland Hurunui gets colder than the coast, and a standard heat pump can struggle when the temperature drops below zero, which is exactly when you want it working. Mitsubishi Hyper Core units are built for that and keep producing heat in genuine frost. It is worth spending a bit more on the right unit out here rather than being disappointed every July.

Summers swing the other way, so the same unit earns its keep again in January.
