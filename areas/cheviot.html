Farm and rural work

Cheviot is farming country, and farm electrical work has its own character: long cable runs to outbuildings, three-phase gear, pump sheds, yard lighting and equipment that has to keep working. We are used to properties where the woolshed is a paddock away from the house and the supply arrangements are not textbook.

Making the trip worth it

Because we are coming from Amberley rather than town, it is worth thinking about what else needs doing while we are there. Plenty of Cheviot customers line up a heat pump, a few dodgy power points and some shed lighting into one visit. It is a better use of everyone's time and it works out cheaper than three separate call-outs.

Heating that suits the district

Most Cheviot homes are heated with a woodburner, and there is nothing wrong with that. The issue is usually that the lounge is roasting while the bedrooms are freezing. A heat transfer system moves that warm air where you actually want it, for a lot less than putting in a second heat source. Where a heat pump does make sense, we fit Hyper Core units that keep working properly on cold mornings.
