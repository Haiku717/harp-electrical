New builds and growing streets

Woodend has taken a lot of new housing over the past few years, and new builds are a big part of what we do there. If your place is going up now, the smart move is planning the heating in alongside the wiring rather than deciding on a heat pump once the gib is on. Ducted systems in particular are far cheaper to install while the ceiling is still open.

Woodend Beach and salt air

Out towards the beach you get the same issue as any coastal settlement: salt in the air is hard on outdoor heat pump units and exposed fittings. Where the outdoor unit goes makes a real difference to how long it lasts, and it is worth a rinse down now and then. We will site it sensibly rather than just putting it on the nearest wall.

Older houses in the township

Closer in there is plenty of older housing where the switchboard has not kept up with the appliances. If your RCD is nuisance tripping or the board still has rewireable fuses, that is worth sorting before you add anything else to it.
