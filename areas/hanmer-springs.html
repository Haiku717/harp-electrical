Heat pumps in Hanmer

Hanmer gets properly cold. Frosts, snow on the tops, and a good number of mornings well below zero through winter. That is exactly where a lot of heat pumps disappoint people, because a standard unit loses output as the temperature drops.

Mitsubishi Hyper Core units are built for it and keep delivering heat in a genuine frost. If you have been told a heat pump will not cut it up here, you were probably being quoted the wrong unit.

Holiday homes and rentals

A lot of Hanmer housing sits empty between visits, which brings its own problems. A cold, closed-up house builds condensation, and you arrive on Friday night to a damp place that takes until Sunday to warm up.

  • Heat pumps you can turn on remotely, so the place is warm when you arrive
  • Lossnay ventilation to keep air moving while nobody is there
  • Heat transfer to spread warmth from the fire into the bedrooms
  • Security cameras so you can check on the place from home
  • Switchboard upgrades and safety checks between tenancies
