Electrical and heating for Amberley homes

Amberley has a real mix of housing: older places around the village that have been added to over the decades, newer subdivisions off the highway, and lifestyle blocks spreading out towards the beach and the Waipara side. They each throw up different jobs.

  • Heat pump supply, installation and servicing, Mitsubishi Electric approved
  • Ducted systems for whole-home heating and cooling
  • Lossnay ventilation and heat transfer for condensation and damp
  • Switchboard upgrades with full RCD protection
  • Repairs, fault finding and safety checks
  • LED lighting, indoor, outdoor and security
  • New builds, extensions and renovations
  • Security cameras for homes and lifestyle blocks

Why being based here matters

Most electricians serving Amberley are driving up from Christchurch or across from Rangiora. That shows up in two ways: what you pay for travel, and how long you wait. We are already here. If we need to come back to finish something or check a fault, that is a short trip, not a scheduling problem.

It also means we know the local housing stock. We have seen what the older cottages around the village tend to have behind the walls, and we know which of the newer builds off the highway were wired with room to add a heat pump later.

Amberley Beach and the coast

Places out towards Amberley Beach cop the salt air, and that is hard on outdoor units, fittings and anything with a metal bracket. It is worth siting an outdoor heat pump unit thoughtfully and checking it more often than you would inland. We factor that in when we quote rather than finding out later.

Business and rural work

We look after shops and businesses along the main road as well as houses, plus the lifestyle blocks and small farms around the district. Sheds, pump sheds, yard lighting and long cable runs are all normal work for us.
