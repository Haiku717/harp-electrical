Lifestyle blocks and small farms

This is lifestyle block country: houses on a few hectares, a shed or two, maybe a stable or a workshop, and often a supply arrangement that has grown organically over the years. The usual jobs are getting power and decent light out to the shed, sorting a board that is carrying more than it should, and dealing with cable runs a lot longer than a town house would ever need.

We do this sort of work constantly, so we are not learning on your job.

Older homes on big sections

A fair few places around Sefton and Ashley are older houses that have been extended over time. Behind the walls that often means two or three eras of wiring joined together, and a switchboard that predates most of the appliances plugged into it. A board upgrade with RCD protection is usually money well spent, and it clears the way to add a heat pump without nuisance tripping.

Heating without a second fire

Most homes out this way run a woodburner. A heat transfer system takes the heat pooling at the ceiling in that room and puts it into the bedrooms, which is far cheaper than a second heat source and makes a real difference on a frosty morning.
