Beach houses and damp

A lot of the housing at Leithfield Beach started life as a bach. Those places tend to be older, lightly built, single glazed, and shut up for weeks at a time. Open the door after a fortnight away and you know about it. That combination produces condensation and mould faster than almost anything else.

Ventilation is usually the right answer there rather than more heating. A Lossnay system keeps air moving through the house while you are not in it, which stops the damp building up in the first place. Paired with a heat pump you get a place that is warm when you arrive and dry when you are not there.

Salt air on the coast

Being close to the beach is hard on outdoor equipment. Salt gets into heat pump outdoor units, fittings, brackets and anything exposed. Where a unit goes matters more here than it does inland, and so does giving it a wash down now and then. We will site it sensibly and tell you what to keep an eye on.

Holiday homes and rentals

If the place is rented out or used as a holiday home, ventilation and heating also matter for Healthy Homes compliance. We can look at what you have and tell you plainly where it stands.
