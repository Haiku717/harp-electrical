Wineries and cellar doors

The Waipara Valley has a lot of commercial electrical work that does not look like commercial electrical work. Cellar doors, tasting rooms, cool stores, packing sheds and irrigation. It tends to involve three-phase supply, long runs out to outbuildings, and gear that cannot afford to go down in the middle of vintage. We are set up for that, and being nearby means a fault gets looked at the same day rather than next week.

Older homes and lifestyle blocks

A lot of properties around Waipara are older houses on decent sized sections, often extended over the years by whoever owned them at the time. That usually means a switchboard carrying more than it was built for and wiring of several different vintages. A board upgrade is often the sensible first move, and it makes adding a heat pump straightforward afterwards.

Cold nights, hot summers

The valley gets sharp frosts in winter and properly hot dry summers. That is exactly the case for a heat pump rather than more heaters: one system that takes the edge off the mornings and cools the house in February. We size units for the actual conditions out here, not a Christchurch average.
