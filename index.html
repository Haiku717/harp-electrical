Common questions

Good to know

Do you charge a call-out fee to Hurunui towns?

No. We are based in Amberley, so Waipara, Leithfield, Waikari and the rest of the Hurunui district are all local to us. You are not paying for a sparky to drive out from Christchurch and back.

Are you a registered electrician?

Yes. All electrical work is carried out by a registered electrician, tested and certified, with the paperwork supplied on completion. We are fully insured.

Which heat pumps do you install?

We are a Mitsubishi Electric approved installer, covering wall-mounted units, ducted systems, heat transfer and Lossnay ventilation. Being approved means the job meets the manufacturer's standard and your warranty is fully protected.

Can I pay a heat pump off over time?

Yes. We offer Q Card finance with up to 18 months interest free on jobs over $2,000, which covers most heat pump installs, lighting upgrades and rewires. See our finance page for the full terms.

Will I get a price before you start?

Yes. You get the number up front, so there are no call-out surprises and no padded invoice at the end. If we find something unexpected once we are into the job, you hear about it before we do the extra work.

How long does a heat pump install take?

A straightforward wall-mounted unit is usually done in a day. Ducted systems and multi-room installs take longer, and you will get a clear timeframe with your quote before anything starts.
