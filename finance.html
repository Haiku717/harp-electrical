Finance options

Q Card finance with up to 18 months interest free on jobs over ,000. Here are the full terms, including the bits people usually find out later.

18 months interest free

A heat pump, a switchboard upgrade or a full rewire is not a small spend, and it usually arrives at an inconvenient moment. Q Card finance lets you get the job done now and pay it off over time, with up to 18 months interest free on jobs over $2,000.

What it covers

  • Heat pump supply and installation, including ducted systems
  • Ventilation and heat transfer systems
  • Switchboard upgrades
  • LED lighting upgrades
  • Full and partial rewires
  • New build and renovation electrical work

How it works

Q Card is a credit card run by Q Mastercard. If you already have one, you can use it with us straight away. If you do not, you apply to Q Card directly and they decide whether to approve you. We do not handle your application or see your financial details.

Once you are approved, you use the card to pay for the job and it goes onto a Long Term Finance plan with the interest free period applied.

The terms, in plain English

We would rather you saw all of this up front than found it later:

  • Interest free period: up to 18 months on Long Term Finance
  • Minimum spend: $2,000
  • Annual account fee: $50
  • Establishment fee: $55 on your first Long Term Finance transaction, $35 on any after that
  • Minimum monthly payment: 3% of the closing balance, or $10, whichever is higher
  • After the interest free period ends: the standard rate of 28.95% p.a. applies to whatever is left owing

That last point is the one that catches people out. Interest free is genuinely interest free, but only until the term is up. If you can clear the balance inside the 18 months it is a good deal. If you cannot, the rate that kicks in afterwards is a high one, so it pays to be realistic about the monthly payments before you commit.

Fees and rates are set by Q Card, not by us, and can change. Check the current terms with them when you apply.

Applying

You apply directly with Q Card at qcard.co.nz. Lending criteria, fees, terms and conditions apply.

If you are not sure whether finance is the right move for your job, ring us and ask. Sometimes it is, and sometimes the job is small enough that it is not worth the fees.
