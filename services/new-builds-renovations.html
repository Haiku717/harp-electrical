New builds & renovations

Full electrical fit-out from first pre-wire to final certification, with the heating planned in from the start instead of bolted on at the end.

We handle the full electrical fit-out for new homes, extensions and renovations across North Canterbury, from the first pre-wire through to final fit-off and certification. Because we do heating as well, the heat pump or ducted system gets planned in from the start rather than bolted on at the end.

New builds

  • Pre-wire and rough-in, coordinated with the build programme
  • Switchboard supply and installation
  • Lighting design and layout, including outdoor and security lighting
  • Power, data and TV points wherever you actually need them
  • Heat pump, ducted system and ventilation wiring planned in early
  • Final fit-off, testing and certification

Renovations and extensions

Renovation work is a different beast to a new build. You are tying new wiring into whatever is already there, which is often decades old and not always what the previous owner said it was. We check what we are connecting to before we extend it, and if the existing board or wiring will not take the extra load you hear about it at quote stage, not halfway through.

One trade instead of two

On most jobs the heating and the electrical are two separate contractors who have to be booked around each other. We do both, so the ducting, the wiring and the switchboard capacity get planned together. Fewer people to chase, and fewer gaps for something to fall through.

Working with your builder

We are used to fitting into a build programme and turning up when we said we would. If your builder needs us on site on a particular day to keep the job moving, that is the arrangement we work to.
