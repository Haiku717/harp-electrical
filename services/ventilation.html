Ventilation & heat transfer

Lossnay ventilation and heat transfer systems that cut condensation, clear the damp and move warmth into the cold rooms.

If your windows stream with condensation every morning, or the back bedroom smells damp no matter what you do, the problem is usually moisture and stale air rather than heating. Ventilation and heat transfer deal with the cause instead of the symptom.

Lossnay ventilation

Lossnay is Mitsubishi Electric's balanced ventilation system. It pulls stale, damp air out of the house and brings filtered fresh air in, recovering the heat from the outgoing air on the way past. That last part matters: you get fresh air without throwing away the heating you have just paid for.

  • Cuts condensation on windows, and the mould that follows it
  • Filters what comes in, which helps if you are on a gravel road or you get hay fever
  • Recovers heat from the outgoing air, so it works with your heating rather than against it
  • Runs quietly and continuously in the background

Heat transfer systems

A heat transfer system takes the warm air that pools at the ceiling in the room with the fire or the heat pump, and moves it to the rooms that never warm up. It is a much cheaper way to take the chill off bedrooms than installing a second heat pump, and for a lot of Hurunui homes with a woodburner in the lounge it is exactly the right answer.

Damp homes in North Canterbury

Plenty of houses out here are older, single glazed and built before anyone thought much about airflow. Add a woodburner, a full clothes horse and a couple of long showers and there is a lot of moisture with nowhere to go. Ventilation is usually the cheapest real improvement you can make to a house like that, and it makes the heating you already have go further.

If you rent the place out, ventilation also counts towards your Healthy Homes obligations.
