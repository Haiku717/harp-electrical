Ducted heat pumps

One hidden system heating and cooling every room, with nothing on your walls. Designed, installed and certified by your local Amberley team.

A ducted heat pump heats and cools the whole house from one system, with nothing on the walls except a discreet vent in each room's ceiling. If you want even warmth through every bedroom rather than one hot lounge and a cold hallway, this is the setup.

How it works

The indoor unit sits hidden in the ceiling space or under the floor. Insulated ducting runs from it to a vent in each room, and one controller sets the temperature. From inside the house you see the vents and nothing else.

  • Even heating and cooling through the whole home
  • Nothing mounted on your walls, so it suits a renovation where you care about the look
  • Zoning, so you are not paying to heat bedrooms nobody is in during the day
  • Quieter than a wall unit, because the working part is not in the room with you
  • Cooling in summer from the same system

Best fitted at the right moment

Ducted systems are far easier and cheaper to install while a house is being built, or while the ceiling is open during a renovation. If you are planning either, talk to us early. We can work the ducting into the plan alongside the rest of the electrical fit-out instead of retrofitting it afterwards.

Retrofits into existing homes are absolutely doable too, as long as there is enough roof space to run the ducting. We come and look before quoting, so you get a real answer rather than a guess over the phone.

Sized for North Canterbury

We get genuinely cold mornings and hot dry summers out here, and a lot of the housing stock is older and leakier than the brochure assumes. We size ducted systems against the house you actually have, not an ideal one.
