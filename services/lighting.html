LED lighting

Indoor, outdoor and security lighting, plus LED upgrades that cut what you pay to light the place. Homes, sheds and businesses.

Good lighting changes how a room feels, and swapping old halogens for LEDs is one of the few upgrades that pays you back every month on the power bill. We design, supply and install lighting for homes, sheds and businesses across North Canterbury.

What we do

  • LED downlight upgrades, replacing power-hungry halogens
  • Kitchen, bathroom and living room lighting design for renovations
  • Outdoor and garden lighting, including driveways and entranceways
  • Security and sensor lighting for houses, sheds and yards
  • Shed, workshop and farm building lighting
  • Commercial and office lighting, including high-bay for large spaces

Why swap to LED

An old halogen downlight burns around 50 watts. The LED that replaces it does the same job on about 5. In a kitchen with ten downlights that adds up quickly, and LEDs last years longer, so you are not up a ladder replacing bulbs every winter. They also run cool, which matters where downlights sit close to ceiling insulation.

Lighting for rural properties

A lot of what we do out here is not house lighting at all. It is getting decent light into a woolshed, lighting a long driveway so you can find the gate in the dark, or putting sensor floods on a set of farm buildings. Rural jobs have their own quirks, like long cable runs and outbuildings on separate supplies, and we deal with those regularly.

Planning a renovation?

Lighting is much cheaper to get right while the walls or ceiling are open. If you are renovating, get us in at the planning stage rather than at the end, and we will lay out switching and light positions that actually suit how you use the room.
