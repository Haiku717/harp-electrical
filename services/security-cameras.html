Security cameras & CCTV

Camera systems for homes, sheds, yards and businesses, installed by a registered electrician and set up on your phone before we go.

A camera system is worth having long before anything goes wrong. We install and set up CCTV and security cameras for homes, sheds, yards and businesses across the Hurunui, and get it working properly on your phone before we leave.

What we install

  • HD camera systems for homes, covering entrances, driveways and outbuildings
  • Rural and lifestyle block coverage: sheds, yards, gateways and machinery
  • Commercial systems for shops, workshops and storage
  • Recording set up so you can go back and find the footage that matters
  • Remote viewing on your phone, tested and working before we go
  • Sensor and security lighting alongside the cameras

Why it matters out here

Rural properties are spread out, often unattended during the day, and a long way from the nearest neighbour. That is a hard combination. Cameras on the gate, the shed and the machinery mean you can check the place from your phone, and if something does happen you have footage the police can actually use rather than a vague description.

Set up so you will actually use it

Plenty of camera systems get installed and then never looked at, because nobody ever got the app working. We set the whole thing up with you, show you how to pull up a recording, and make sure you can see the cameras on your phone before we pack up.

Done by a registered electrician

Cameras need power, and often that means new circuits, outdoor-rated cabling and weatherproof connections. It gets done properly, tested and certified, the same as any other electrical work we do.
