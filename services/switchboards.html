Switchboard upgrades

Swap an ageing fuse board for a modern switchboard with full RCD protection. Certified, safety tested, and usually done in a day.

If your board still has ceramic fuses with fuse wire in them, or the lights dim when the jug goes on, it is time. A modern switchboard with RCD protection is the single biggest safety upgrade most older homes can make.

Why old boards are a problem

An old fuse board does one thing: it cuts the power if a circuit draws too much current. What it does not do is protect a person. An RCD detects electricity leaking to earth, through a frayed cord or a person touching something live, and cuts the power in milliseconds. That is the difference between a fright and a fatality.

  • Ceramic or rewireable fuses replaced with modern circuit breakers
  • Full RCD protection across your circuits
  • Extra capacity for a heat pump, an oven, a workshop or a shed
  • Clearly labelled circuits, so you know what turns off what
  • Certified and safety tested, with the paperwork supplied

Common in older North Canterbury housing

A lot of the villas and older cottages around Amberley, Waikari and Cheviot are still running boards that were installed decades ago, back when a house had a fraction of the appliances it has now. Add a heat pump, a dishwasher and a couple of heaters to a board that was never designed for it and you get nuisance tripping at best.

What is involved

Most switchboard upgrades are a one day job. Your power is off while we work, so we agree a day that suits you, and we tell you up front what it will cost before we start. If we find something behind the board that needs sorting, you hear about it before we do it, not on the invoice.

Switchboard upgrades qualify for Q Card finance if you would rather spread the cost.
