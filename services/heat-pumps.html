Heat pump installation

Sized properly, installed tidily and certified by a registered electrician. We are a Mitsubishi Electric approved installer based right here in Amberley.

A heat pump is the cheapest way to heat a New Zealand home, but only if it is the right size for the room and installed properly. We are a Mitsubishi Electric approved installer based in Amberley, so you get the unit sized correctly, mounted where it will actually work, and signed off by a registered electrician.

What we install

We fit Mitsubishi Electric wall-mounted heat pumps across the Hurunui and North Canterbury, from a single unit in the living room to a whole-house setup.

  • High-wall units for living rooms, bedrooms and offices
  • Hyper Core models built for cold Canterbury mornings, which keep putting out heat when the temperature drops below zero
  • Multi-split systems running several indoor units off one outdoor unit
  • Replacement of old or failed units, reusing existing pipework where it is sound
  • Servicing, filter cleans and repairs on units we did not install

Why the sizing matters

An undersized heat pump runs flat out and never quite warms the room. An oversized one short-cycles, costs more to run and wears out sooner. We measure the space and take the insulation, glazing and aspect into account, then recommend the unit that suits it rather than the one we happen to have in the van.

What being approved actually means

Mitsubishi Electric only lists installers who work to their standard. That protects your manufacturer warranty, which is the part most people do not think about until something goes wrong three winters later. It also means the refrigerant work, the brackets, the drainage and the electrical connection are all done the way the maker intended.
