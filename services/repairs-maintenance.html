Diagnosed properly

Repairs & fault finding

Tripping circuits, dead outlets and failing switches diagnosed properly, repaired and made safe. Across the Hurunui and North Canterbury.

When a circuit keeps tripping or half the house has gone dark, you want someone who will find the actual cause rather than reset the breaker and leave. We diagnose, repair and certify electrical faults across the Hurunui and North Canterbury.

What we get called out to

  • Circuits that trip repeatedly, or an RCD that will not stay in
  • Dead power points, dead lights and sections of the house with no power
  • Burning smells, scorched outlets and switches that feel warm
  • Flickering lights and appliances that cut out under load
  • Hot water and oven circuit faults
  • Damage after a storm, a leak, or a rodent getting into the ceiling

Finding the real cause

Most repeat faults are a symptom of something else: moisture getting somewhere it should not, a failing appliance dragging a circuit down, or wiring that has been quietly deteriorating for years. We test properly to isolate it, tell you what we found in plain terms, and give you the price to fix it before we do the work.

Safety checks and maintenance

We also do scheduled maintenance and safety inspections, which is worth doing if you are buying an older property, renting one out, or running a business with equipment you cannot afford to have go down. For rentals it is a sensible way to show you have taken reasonable care.

Local, so we get there sooner

Being based in Amberley means a fault out in the Hurunui gets looked at without waiting on someone to drive up from Christchurch. Tell us what it is doing when you call, and if it is something that needs making safe straight away, say so and we will treat it that way.
